Privacy for parents

Last updated: 22 October 2026

JUNO is a game for children, made by RazTech Innovations in Sweden. We built it so that it collects nothing about your child or your family. There is no account, no tracking and no ads, and the game sends nothing over the internet.

What stays on your device

Your child's Juno and progress: the pet, its name, stars, coins, food, the garden, the room and the settings. They are saved on your iPhone or iPad, and kept in step between your family's own devices through your iCloud account, like any app's iCloud data; they are included in your own device backup too. They never reach us or anyone else. The name your child types never leaves your own devices and iCloud.

What we do not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no email address, no phone number.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifier, no "allow tracking" prompt.
  • No ads.
  • No chat, no friends list, no way to talk to strangers.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.
  • The game does not send anything over the internet.

Reading aloud

So that children who cannot read yet can play, GrandPa Tintin reads the game's messages aloud. His voice is recorded clips that come with the app, made in advance on our own computer, and none of them contain your child's name or the Juno's name. A line without a clip is read by your device's own voice, on the device. No text or sound is sent anywhere.

Purchases

Anything for sale is bought through Apple, behind a parental gate that a grown-up has to answer first. We never see your payment details or who you are; Apple handles the purchase under Apple's privacy policy.

The Parent Area

A grown-up opens it with the ⚙︎ button and the parental gate. There you can set the language, reading aloud, sounds, bedtime, a gentle break reminder, and how long Memory Garden cards stay face up, and you can start the game over.

Children

JUNO is made for children aged 6 to 10 and treats every player as a child. Because it collects no personal information at all, there is nothing to ask consent for, share or sell.

Age range

Some places, such as Texas, ask apps to check a player's age range. Where the law asks for it, JUNO uses Apple's own way of asking: a grown-up can choose to share an age range such as “under 13”, never a birthday. JUNO keeps the answer only while the game is open, never saves or sends it, and the game is the same at every age. If a grown-up chooses not to share, the game waits until a grown-up says yes.

Deleting your child's data

Choose Start the game over in the Parent Area to remove the Juno and all progress, or delete the app to remove everything. Purchases stay with your Apple account.

This website

This site has no cookies, no analytics and no forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If the game ever changes what it does with data, we update this page first and change the date at the top.

Questions

Write to us at raztechsupport@icloud.com. We answer as soon as we can.
